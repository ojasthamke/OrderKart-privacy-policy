Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1 August 2026 Last Updated: 31 August 2026 Governing Law: Indian DPDP Act 2023 & IT Rules 2021
Privacy Summary: OrderKart collects only the essential customer and delivery address details required to fulfill scheduled vegetable and grocery orders. We do NOT collect background GPS location, photos, contacts, microphone audio, or debit/credit card numbers.

1. Overview & Commitment

OrderKart ("we", "us", "our") values your trust and is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which OrderKart collects, uses, maintains, and discloses personal information collected from customers ("you", "your") using the OrderKart mobile application and ordering services. We operate in strict compliance with applicable Indian data protection legislation, including the Information Technology Act 2000, IT Rules 2021, and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

2. Information We Collect

To enable efficient home delivery of fresh agricultural produce and daily essentials, we collect the following limited information:

  • Personal Identifiers: Customer full name, primary mobile phone number, alternate phone number (if provided), and unique Customer Member Code allocated by the store.
  • Delivery & Route Details: House/flat number, street/road name, sub-road, and registered area name mapped within our store delivery routes in Pune, Maharashtra.
  • Order & Transaction Records: Details of items ordered, quantities, unit prices, delivery schedules, order dates, and total billed amounts.
  • Device & Notification Data: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device registration tokens used strictly to transmit transactional notifications regarding order confirmation, dispatch, and delivery arrival.
  • Security Credentials: Authentication tokens and password hashes stored on your device using hardware-backed secure storage.

3. Permissions Clarification (What We DO NOT Collect)

To safeguard your privacy, our mobile application requests minimal system permissions:

  • No Background GPS Location: Addresses are selected via predefined delivery routes; the app does NOT access or track your live GPS coordinates.
  • No Camera or Photos: The app does not access your camera, gallery, or media storage.
  • No Device Contacts: We do not access or upload your address book or contact lists.
  • No Microphone or Bluetooth: No audio recording or nearby device tracking is performed.
  • No Financial Payment Card Numbers: All payments are Pay on Delivery (Cash on Delivery or on-the-spot UPI QR code transfer). We never collect or process credit card numbers or banking PINs.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your information is processed strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Assembling, sorting, packaging, and delivering your ordered goods to your doorstep.
  • Sending critical transactional notifications regarding order dispatch, route delivery times, and store schedule alerts.
  • Authenticating customer login and preventing brute-force unauthorized access attempts through automated client rate-limiting.
  • Providing customer support and resolving grievance inquiries via official WhatsApp and phone helplines.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with reputable technology providers under strict security and confidentiality obligations:

  • Supabase Inc. (Cloud Database & Authentication): Data is stored in secure cloud servers with TLS 1.3 transit encryption and AES-256 rest encryption.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Delivers real-time push notifications to your Android device.
  • Google Fonts: Provides clean typography rendered within the mobile user interface.

6. Data Security & Technical Diagnostics

All network communications between the mobile application and server APIs are encrypted using modern HTTPS / TLS 1.3 protocols. Authentication tokens are safeguarded using platform-native secure keystores. Cloud backend systems (Supabase and Google Cloud) automatically record standard technical diagnostic logs (such as IP addresses, API request timestamps, and performance metrics) strictly for security protection, network integrity, and uptime monitoring.

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, we implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion Rights

You maintain complete ownership of your personal data. You may request permanent deletion of your online app account at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Open OrderKart > My Profile > Information & Support > Delete Account.
  • Web Portal / Email Request: Visit our Account Deletion Page or email supportorderkart@gmail.com.
  • Data Purged: Online login credentials, password hashes, active sessions, and FCM notification tokens are permanently erased from cloud databases.
  • Statutory Retention: Past completed order invoices, tax receipts, and merchant ledger entries are retained in the shop's local point-of-sale system as required by Indian commercial, GST, and income tax regulations.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect operational, regulatory, or technical changes. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp. Continued use of the OrderKart application following the posting of changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
