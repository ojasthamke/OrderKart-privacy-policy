OrderKart Privacy Policy

Entity: OrderKart Fresh Foods & Essentials Effective Date: 1 August 2026 Last Updated: 31 August 2026 Version: 2.0 (Verified Final)
Privacy Commitment: OrderKart respects your personal privacy. We collect only the bare minimum personal data required to register your account, route your grocery orders, and provide customer support. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

1. Introduction & Operating Entity

This Privacy Policy outlines how OrderKart Fresh Foods & Essentials ("OrderKart", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal information obtained from users ("you" or "customer") of the OrderKart customer mobile application and related delivery services operating in Pune, Maharashtra, India.

OrderKart operates as a single-seller Inventory E-Commerce Entity delivering fresh vegetables, fruits, and household goods. We do not operate a multi-vendor marketplace.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential personal details you provide during registration and order processing:

  • Contact Identification: Full Name and Primary 10-digit Mobile Phone Number (used for OTP-based authentication, order status updates, and delivery verification).
  • Delivery Address: House/Flat Number, Building Name, Society/Colony, Street/Road name, and Assigned Delivery Area within Pune.
  • Optional Details: Alternate contact number and dietary preferences if voluntarily provided.
  • Device Diagnostics & Push Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration tokens to deliver real-time order confirmation, out-for-delivery alerts, and delivery schedule notifications.
What We DO NOT Collect:
  • ❌ We DO NOT collect continuous background GPS location or real-time location tracking.
  • ❌ We DO NOT access your camera, photo gallery, or device media.
  • ❌ We DO NOT access your phone address book or contacts.
  • ❌ We DO NOT store debit/credit card numbers or banking passwords (payments are processed via Pay on Delivery / UPI QR).

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To authenticate your login securely via mobile OTP and PIN verification.
  • To process, pack, and deliver your fresh vegetable orders to your registered address.
  • To communicate order status, dispatch timing, and delivery schedules via push notifications, SMS, or WhatsApp.
  • To maintain accurate accounting ledgers and generate statutory tax invoices in accordance with Indian tax laws.

4. Backend Cloud Architecture & Third-Party Processors

OrderKart employs industry-standard cloud infrastructure to guarantee data integrity:

  • Supabase Cloud: Encrypted PostgreSQL database instance with Row Level Security (RLS) and SSL/TLS 1.3 encryption for secure account and order synchronization.
  • Google Firebase: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) used solely for delivering operational transactional push notifications to your device.

5. Data Retention & Statutory Accounting Obligations

Personal profile information and authentication credentials are maintained while your account remains active.

Under Section 44AA of the Indian Income Tax Act 1961 and Section 35 of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act 2017, completed commercial transaction records, tax invoices, and sales receipts must be retained in our local shop records for statutory audit periods (up to 8 consecutive years).

6. Account Deletion & User Rights

You have the absolute right to delete your OrderKart account at any time:

  • Instant In-App Deletion: Open the OrderKart app > My Profile > Information & Support > Tap Delete Account. Your cloud credentials, password hashes, and FCM tokens will be erased instantly.
  • External Web Deletion Portal: Visit our dedicated Account Deletion Portal or email supportorderkart@gmail.com. Web deletion requests are verified and processed within 24 to 48 hours.

Business Identity & Operations

About OrderKart Fresh Foods & Essentials

OrderKart is Pune's specialized local farm-to-table fresh produce and daily grocery delivery service. Founded to bridge local Pune households directly with the Pune Agriculture Produce Market Committee (APMC) and regional farms, OrderKart delivers unmatched freshness, wholesale market rates, and honest weights.

Core Operational Pillars:

  • Direct Sourcing: All vegetables and fruits are freshly procured every morning directly from farmers and the Pune APMC market.
  • Transparent Metric Pricing: Clear per-kilogram and fractional metric rates with transparent checkout price locking.
  • Hygienic Morning Sorting: Hand-picked, sorted, and packed in clean facilities before morning and evening delivery runs.
  • Pay on Delivery: Inspect your fresh items at your doorstep before completing payment via Cash or UPI.
Pricing, Delivery & Refund Rules

Store Operations & Customer Terms

1. Delivery Charges & Thresholds

  • Orders ₹300 and above: FREE Doorstep Delivery.
  • Orders below ₹300: Flat ₹30 nominal delivery fee.
  • Daily Cutoff Time: 23:59 IST (Orders placed before midnight are packed fresh for next-day delivery).

2. Five-Rupee Ceiling Rounding

To eliminate loose coin friction during Cash on Delivery, OrderKart applies standard 5-rupee ceiling rounding: (Grand Total ÷ 5).ceil() × 5. The exact unrounded total and rounded billing amount are displayed with 100% transparency at checkout.

3. Return, Refund & Quality Guarantee

Because produce is perishable, customers can inspect all items at the time of delivery. If any item fails to meet quality standards, you may refuse the item on the spot, and the bill total will be adjusted immediately before payment.
