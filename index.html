About OrderKart

Direct Inventory Model • Honest Freshness

OrderKart is an Inventory E-Commerce Entity. We own, inspect, grade, package, and fulfill our entire fresh produce inventory directly to ensure peak quality.

🌅

Morning Market Sourcing

We source directly from Agriculture Produce Market Committees (APMC) and regional farms in the early morning hours to bring you maximum freshness and wholesale pricing.

⚖️

5-Rupee Smart Rounding

No more searching for change! Grand totals are rounded up to the nearest ₹5 multiple so you and our delivery executives experience frictionless cash/UPI handoffs.

How OrderKart Works

From daily wholesale auctions to your kitchen counter in four simple steps.

1️⃣

Select & Place Order

Browse live daily catalog prices in the OrderKart app. Add items to your cart and place your order before the 23:59 cutoff.

2️⃣

Harvest & Quality Sorting

Our team sources fresh morning lots, performs strict quality inspections, and sorts and weighs your items under hygienic conditions.

3️⃣

Doorstep Delivery

Our route delivery executives bring your fresh vegetables directly to your registered house/flat in Pune during morning delivery slots.

4️⃣

Inspect & Settle

Inspect your produce at your doorstep and pay comfortably using Cash on Delivery or scanning our official UPI QR code.
